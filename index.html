02 / THE CORE SAGA

Eight films. One long spell.

The core Harry Potter story is an eight-film series. The final book, Deathly Hallows, was split into two films.

WATCHING NOW?

One good rule:
don't Google the ending.

These films are built around discoveries. Search results can casually spoil a 20-year-old twist in a single headline, so let the story reveal itself on its own terms.

05 / THE FOUR HOUSES

Where would the Hat put you?

Four houses, four values — and a lot more nuance than internet quizzes make it sound.

♞

Gryffindor

Bravery, nerve, daring and chivalry.

Think: courage before comfort.
蛇

Slytherin

Ambition, resourcefulness, determination and pride.

Think: make a way where there isn't one.
✦

Ravenclaw

Wit, learning, curiosity and originality.

Think: ask one more question.
✤

Hufflepuff

Loyalty, patience, fairness and hard work.

Think: stay when things get difficult.
06 / A VERY SMALL TIMELINE

How the history connects.

This is the spoiler-light spine of the universe. It explains why old names keep returning.

19th century

Hogwarts grows into legend

The four founders establish the school and its houses. Their values shape Hogwarts for centuries.

1940s

Tom Riddle's rise begins

A brilliant Hogwarts student becomes obsessed with bloodline, power, immortality and the darkest corners of magic.

1981

The night everything changes

Voldemort attacks the Potter family. Harry survives, and the wizarding world gives him a name he never asked for.

1991

Harry enters Hogwarts

A train, a sorting ceremony, two lifelong friendships — and the first visible thread of a much older conflict.

1991–1998

The seven-year story

Each school year expands the world, raises the stakes, and turns childhood mysteries into a war for the future.
